August 3, 2026 · Ibid.

The House Ethics Committee's 20-page report recommends censuring Rep. Chuck Edwards (R-N.C.) for "persistent unprofessional and inappropriate conduct" toward two young female staffers. After 16 interviews and more than 1,500 pages of evidence, investigators found no evidence he propositioned them but documented "lavish and recurrent gifts": over $1,000 in jewelry, designer purses, guns, shoes, a laptop, a KitchenAid mixer, vacations and one-on-one dinners. Edwards' lawyers say he regrets that his conduct created discomfort. With the House in recess, a floor censure vote waits until at least September.
