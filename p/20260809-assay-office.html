At a Sunday cabinet meeting Benjamin Netanyahu said "Israel rejects the 15-point document" put forward by Donald Trump on Gaza, and that the IDF would carry out no withdrawal until Hamas is "genuinely" disarmed, rejecting any "fictitious disarmament." Hamas has tied the handover of heavy weapons to Israel ending all aggression and withdrawing. Trump's Board of Peace announced agreement on the "complete disarmament" of Hamas last month, and far-right minister Itamar Ben Gvir is demanding a fresh cabinet vote to kill the plan.
