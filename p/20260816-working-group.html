August 16, 2026 · Ibid.

US envoy Jared Kushner met Hamas leader Khalil al-Hayya in Egypt on Sunday, the first contact at that level since the 10 October Gaza ceasefire. Board of Peace envoys Nickolay Mladenov and Tony Blair attended alongside Egyptian, Qatari and Turkish mediators. Israel last week rejected the board's 15-point plan, with Prime Minister Netanyahu saying the IDF will not withdraw until Hamas is genuinely disarmed. More than 73,000 people have been killed in Gaza and about 1.9 million displaced, and eight countries issued a joint statement warning that Israel's rejection threatened the peace effort.
