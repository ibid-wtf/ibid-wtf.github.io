The Associated Press reports that U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement plans to issue its officers gloves capable of delivering painful electric shocks. The device is worn on the hand, so the shock travels through an officer's own grip or touch. The purchase is an agency procurement decision made during ICE's mass-deportation enforcement surge; such equipment is typically defended in the vocabulary of less-lethal, compliance and de-escalation tools.
