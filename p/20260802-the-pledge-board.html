An estimated 60,000 Moroccans entered the Spanish enclave of Ceuta in little over 24 hours, and dozens died in the attempt. Around 90 percent had returned to Morocco by Saturday night, with police divers still recovering bodies from the waters around the 19-square-kilometre territory. Spain's government blamed criminal trafficking gangs while opposition politicians blamed its immigration policy; Reform UK's Zia Yusuf visited and told his X followers the men were trying to reach Britain, a claim no one interviewed by the reporter made.
