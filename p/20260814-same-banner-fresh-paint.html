Trump said the USS Abraham Lincoln, at sea more than 250 days without a port call — a US Navy record — has not been deployed long enough: "No, no, no, not nearly enough." The Lincoln is the carrier on whose deck a "Mission Accomplished" banner hung behind President George W. Bush in May 2003. Reports this week documented supply shortages and mental health strain among the carrier's 5,000-plus crew, including accounts of attempts to jump from the ship, which a US official disputed. The administration's listed support staffing aboard is five chaplains, one psychologist, one social worker, three behavioral health technicians, one prevention counselor and one emotional support dog; Senators Richard Blumenthal and Ruben Gallego have demanded metrics and a bipartisan visit.
