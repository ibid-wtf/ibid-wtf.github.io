Low optic.

Coverage

August 15, 2026 · Ibid.

ICE published plans on August 10 to buy $20 million of electric shock gloves from Compliant Technologies, for delivery to officers by March 2027. A Cape Girardeau assistant chief praised the device as "low optic" because it leaves no burn marks or scars, while a pending wrongful death suit alleges a 43-year-old man was shocked 27 times with the gloves, including bursts of 45 and 99 seconds against a manufacturer limit of 15 seconds. The company once called the device a weapon on Instagram before rebranding it as a de-escalation tool; Amnesty International says such devices are readily misused for torture.
