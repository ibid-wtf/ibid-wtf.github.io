Eleven percent leaner.

Eighty Hours

August 3, 2026 · Ibid.

More than 4 million people have lost SNAP food benefits since last July, as monthly enrollment fell from about 42 million to 37 million, an 11% drop. The One Big Beautiful Bill Act extended 80-hours-a-month work-or-volunteer requirements to veterans, homeless people, young adults leaving foster care, parents of teenagers and adults aged 55 to 64; the CBO projects 2.4 million fewer participants per average month through 2034. In Arizona enrollment is roughly half what it was a year ago, and a funding restructuring landing in October shifts millions in costs onto the states.

Source: Over 4 million people are no longer receiving food aid amid sweeping changes to SNAP (NPR)
