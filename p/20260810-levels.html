August 10, 2026 · Ibid.

Brent crude rose to $83.77 a barrel on Aug 10, 2026, about 16 percent above pre-war levels, as Iran kept the Strait of Hormuz closed. Traffic through the strait fell from roughly 130 transits a day before the war to between eight and 15 vessels in early August, per MarineTraffic. Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said the strait stays shut until Washington lifts its naval blockade, halts strikes, eases sanctions and pays reparations; Trump told Axios the US was "low-keying it" and "only semi-negotiating."
