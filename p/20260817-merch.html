Israeli minister Itamar Ben-Gvir said on a podcast episode that surfaced on Sunday that Israeli forces should "kill 30 or 40" people in Gaza every night, and said of Palestinians there: "There are people there who are not worthy of life... Ben-Gvir controls the police and prison services but has no authority to order strikes; Israel votes on 27 October.
