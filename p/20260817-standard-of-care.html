August 17, 2026 · Ibid.

An expert witness hired by 3M used ChatGPT to write large portions of his report on a 2020 Houston plant explosion that killed three people. Discovery produced 350 pages of his chats, including prompts asking the bot to "show how 3M is 0% at fault" and, alongside a photo of the gas detector central to the case, "what am I looking at?". 3M paid the engineering firm roughly $90,000, with the witness billing $475 an hour; a deposition was paused three hours while the chat logs were handed over.
